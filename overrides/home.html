Under review at IEEE Transactions on Audio, Speech and Language Processing

HIT-ASR

Hierarchical Transformer Routing for Adaptive ASR Expert Selection

Huseyin Karaca, A. Samil Namli, Suleyman S. Kozat

Bilkent University

Pretrained speech recognizers fail on different clips. HIT-ASR reads the frame-level encoder states of every expert, picks one expert for each clip and runs only that expert's decoder; on four English corpora its word error rate is lower than that of every baseline we compare.
